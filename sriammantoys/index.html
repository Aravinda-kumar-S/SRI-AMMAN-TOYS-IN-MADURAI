Our Story — Est. 2011

Madurai's Trusted Name in Toys & Gifts Since 2011

Established in 2011, Sri Amman Toys on Munichalai Road, Madurai — located Opposite Aruna Hospital — is a top player in the Toy Wholesalers category in Madurai. This well-known establishment acts as a one-stop destination servicing customers both local and from other parts of Madurai.

Over the course of its journey, this business has established a firm foothold in its industry. The belief that customer satisfaction is as important as their products has helped Sri Amman Toys garner a vast base of customers, which continues to grow by the day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Sri Amman Toys located?

We are located at 18, Munichalai Road, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001 — directly Opposite Aruna Hospital. We are easily accessible from all parts of Madurai city.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday: 10:30 AM – 11:00 PM and Sunday: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM. We accept Cash and UPI payments.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes! Sri Amman Toys is one of Madurai's leading toy wholesalers. We offer bulk/wholesale pricing for schools, event planners, retailers and businesses. Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 92457 17267 to get wholesale rates.

Do you have birthday decorations and balloons?

Absolutely. We stock a wide range of balloon bunches, decoration sets, banners and complete birthday decoration kits for every theme and budget — perfect for home parties, hall functions and school events.

Can I enquire or order through WhatsApp?

Yes! WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. Message us at +91 92457 17267 with your product requirement, quantity and location. Our team will respond promptly with availability and pricing.

Do you sell chocolates and greeting cards?

Yes — we carry popular chocolate brands and gift boxes ideal for return gifts and celebrations, along with greeting cards, gift cards and decorative stickers for birthdays, festivals and special occasions.
