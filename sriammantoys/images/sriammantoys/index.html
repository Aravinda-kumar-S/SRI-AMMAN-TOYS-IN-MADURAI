Our Story — Est. 2011

Madurai's Trusted Name in Toys & Gifts Since 2011

Established in the year 2011, Sri Amman Toys on Munichalai Road, Madurai — located Opposite Aruna Hospital — is a top player in the Toy Wholesalers category in Madurai. This well-known establishment acts as a one-stop destination servicing customers both local and from other parts of Madurai.

Over the course of its journey, this business has established a firm foothold in its industry. The belief that customer satisfaction is as important as their products and services has helped Sri Amman Toys garner a vast base of customers, which continues to grow by the day. Our dedicated staff put in a lot of effort to achieve the common vision — bringing quality toys and gifting items within everyone's reach.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is Sri Amman Toys located?

We are located at 18, Munichalai Road, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001 — Opposite Aruna Hospital. This landmark makes it very easy for first-time visitors to find us. Multiple modes of transport are readily available to reach our store.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday: 10:30 AM – 11:00 PM and Sunday: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM. We aim to serve customers across a wide range of timings to suit busy schedules.

Do you sell wholesale / bulk orders?

Yes — Sri Amman Toys is one of Madurai's leading Toy Wholesalers. We supply toys, birthday decorations, party supplies and gift items in bulk to shops, schools, event organisers and corporate clients. WhatsApp us at +91 92457 17267 with your requirement for wholesale pricing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash and UPI payments for a quick and convenient checkout experience. Both retail and wholesale customers can pay using either method.

Do you have birthday decorations and balloons?

Absolutely. We stock a wide range of balloon bunches, decoration sets, banners and complete birthday decoration kits for every theme and budget — perfect for home parties, hall functions and school events.

Can I enquire or order through WhatsApp?

Yes! WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. Message us at +91 92457 17267 with your product requirement, quantity and location. Our team will respond promptly with availability and pricing.

Do you sell chocolates and greeting cards?

Yes — we carry popular chocolate brands and gift boxes ideal for return gifts and celebrations, along with greeting cards, gift cards and decorative stickers for birthdays, festivals and special occasions.

Are you a retail shop only or also wholesale?

We serve both retail and wholesale customers. Individual families, parents and students are welcome for single-item purchases, while schools, event planners and retailers can place bulk orders at wholesale rates. We are recognised as a leading Toy Wholesaler in Madurai.
