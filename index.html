About Sri Amman Toys & Chocolates

Sri Amman Toys & Chocolates is a trusted wholesale supplier in Madurai with over 15 years of successful experience. We supply small toys, balloons, prize items, stickers & cards, chocolates, confectionery products and many more at wholesale prices.

Customers can visit our store and purchase directly. If you are unable to visit, simply place your order by 📞 Phone or 💬 WhatsApp.

We carefully pack every order and dispatch it through the most suitable transport service to ensure safe & timely delivery to your location.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What products does Sri Amman Toys & Chocolates supply at wholesale prices?

We supply small toys, balloons, prize items, stickers & cards, popular chocolates (Dairy Milk, KitKat, Gems, Chupa Chups), wafers, confectionery products, birthday decorations, and price tags at competitive wholesale prices.

Where is your store located in Madurai? Can I visit in person?

We are located at 18, Munichalai Road, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001 — directly Opposite Old Arun Hospital. Customers are welcome to visit our store directly and purchase products.

How can I place an order if I am unable to visit the store?

If you are unable to visit, simply place your order by Phone or WhatsApp at +91 92457 17267. We share photo catalogues and handle orders quickly online.

How are products packed and delivered to my location?

We carefully and hygienically pack every order and dispatch it through the most suitable local transport service to ensure safe and timely delivery right to your location.

Do you supply bulk orders for shopkeepers, line salesmen, school canteens, and repacking companies?

Yes! With over 15+ years of experience, we specialize in bulk wholesale orders for shopkeepers, line salesmen, school canteens, repacking companies, return gift buyers, event planners and corporate functions across Madurai and nearby districts.

What are your store business hours and payment modes?

We are open Monday to Saturday: 10:30 AM – 10:00 PM and Sunday: 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM. We accept Cash & UPI payments for your convenience.
